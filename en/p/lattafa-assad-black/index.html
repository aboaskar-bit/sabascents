About this fragrance

Asad by Lattafa is a warm Oriental men's fragrance that opens with black pepper and a touch of pineapple with tobacco, then moves to a heart of coffee, iris and patchouli. Its base of amber, vanilla and dry woods gives it a clearly masculine presence suited to evenings and cool weather.
