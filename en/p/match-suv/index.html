About this fragrance

A refreshing men's fragrance that begins with bergamot, cardamom and lavender, then rises into warm spices of cinnamon and black pepper with vetiver, settling on vanilla, tonka and cedarwood. It gives energy and freshness with a warm, mysterious touch.
