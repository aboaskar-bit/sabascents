About this fragrance

9 PM Night Out is a new Extrait edition in the 9 PM line, opening with dragon fruit, apple and cognac with lavender. It turns to the warmth of toffee, cardamom and suede, then settles on tonka, woods and amber with a strong, long-lasting trail.
