About this fragrance

An oriental, bakhoor-style fragrance that opens with the freshness of pineapple and bergamot with a touch of pink pepper, then warms up with dry amber and caramel. It closes on an earthy, smoky base of patchouli, vetiver, leather and incense, suited to majlis gatherings and cool evenings.
