About this fragrance

A newer, fresher take on Arrogate Pink, with a clearer presence of rose. It opens with orange blossom and neroli with a creamy almond touch, then a heart of apricot, violet and jasmine, and settles on sandalwood and musk with a calm warmth.

Highlights
