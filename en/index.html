FAQ

Questions & answers

Where is Sabascents based?

Sabascents is an original Arabian perfume store in Türkiye. We take orders on WhatsApp and Instagram and deliver across Türkiye.

How do I order?

Open the perfume’s page and tap “Order on WhatsApp” — a ready message with the perfume and size opens. Send it and we’ll complete the order with you.

Are the perfumes original?

Yes, every perfume is original from its brand: Assaf, Laverne, Ibraq, Lattafa, Reef, Match, OSMA, Alezz Oud, Dkhoon Emirates, Afnan, Rasasi.

What currency are prices in?

All prices are in Turkish lira (₺).

Which brands do you carry?

Assaf, Laverne, Ibraq, Lattafa, Reef, Match, OSMA, Alezz Oud, Dkhoon Emirates, Afnan, Rasasi.
