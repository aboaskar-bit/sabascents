Frequently asked questions

How do I order?

On any perfume page tap “Order on WhatsApp”; a ready message with the perfume and size opens. Send it and we’ll complete the order with you. You can also order via Instagram messages at @sabascents.

Do you deliver within Türkiye?

Yes, we deliver across Türkiye. Shipping details are sent to you on WhatsApp based on your city.

Are the perfumes original?

Yes, every product is original from its brand.

What currency are prices in?

Turkish lira (₺). Prices may change with availability.

What does “Sold out” mean?

The product isn’t available right now; message us and we’ll let you know when it arrives.

Where do the ratings come from?

Ratings are quoted from Fragrantica and similar sites, with a link to the source and the number of voters; the opinion summary is written in our own words.
